Unbit for macOS

Open BitLocker drives
on your Mac.

Plug in an encrypted USB drive, enter the password or recovery key, and browse your files. Read-only. No extra drivers.

Download for Mac

Version 1.0.1 · macOS 13 or later · Apple silicon & Intel

Read-only by design

Nothing is ever written to your drive, so your data stays exactly as it was.

No extra drivers

No kernel extensions and no third-party file systems. It's a single notarized app.

Lives in your menu bar

One click away when you need it. Updates install automatically.

Install in a minute

  1. 1
    Drag to Applications

    Open the DMG and drag Unbit into your Applications folder.

  2. 2
    Grant Full Disk Access

    Unbit walks you through it. You only do this once.

  3. 3
    Plug in your drive

    Click the menu bar icon and enter your password or recovery key.
