Meet Unbit

Open BitLocker-encrypted USB drives on your Mac. Read-only, with no extra drivers. Just enter the password or recovery key.

Download for macOS How it works

Version 1.0.1 · macOS 13 or later · Apple silicon & Intel · Notarized by Apple

Read-only by design

Nothing is ever written to your drive, so your files stay exactly as they were.

No extra drivers

No kernel extensions and no third-party file systems. It's a single app, notarized by Apple.

Lives in your menu bar

It's one click away when you need it, and updates install automatically.

Up and running in a minute

  1. 1Drag to Applications

    Open the DMG and drag Unbit into your Applications folder.

  2. 2Grant Full Disk Access

    Unbit walks you through it. You only do this once.

  3. 3Plug in your drive

    Click the menu bar icon and enter the password or recovery key.
